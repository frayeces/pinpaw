Hapus akun & data — PinPaw: Kucing & Ikan

Akun hanya ada kalau kamu pernah masuk dengan Google di ☁️ Simpan cloud. Tanpa itu, semua progres hanya tersimpan di HP-mu dan terhapus saat aplikasi dihapus.

Cara 1 — di dalam game (langsung)

  1. Buka game → ☰ Lainnya → ☁️ Simpan cloud.
  2. Ketuk 🗑️ Hapus akun & data cloud, lalu ketuk sekali lagi untuk konfirmasi.

Cara 2 — lewat email

Kirim email ke fraye.dev@gmail.com dengan judul "Hapus akun PinPaw" dari alamat Gmail yang dipakai untuk masuk. Permintaan diproses paling lambat 30 hari.

Data yang dihapus

Akun Firebase (ID, nama, email), seluruh data progres di cloud, dan nama pemain & skor di papan peringkat. Tidak ada data yang disimpan setelah penghapusan. Progres yang masih ada di HP-mu tidak ikut terhapus.

Delete account & data — PinPaw: Cat & Fish Puzzle

An account only exists if you signed in with Google in ☁️ Cloud save. Otherwise all progress is stored only on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Option 1 — in the game (immediate)

  1. Open the game → ☰ More → ☁️ Cloud save.
  2. Tap 🗑️ Delete account & cloud data, then tap again to confirm.

Option 2 — by email

Email fraye.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete PinPaw account" from the Gmail address you signed in with. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Data deleted

Your Firebase account (ID, name, email), all cloud progress data, and your player name & leaderboard scores. Nothing is retained after deletion. Progress still on your phone is not affected.
